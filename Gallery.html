🧳 Travel Tips

🏛️ Visiting Heritage Sites▼
Wear comfortable shoes for rocky terrain. Visit Sigiriya early morning to avoid crowds. Entry fees apply — carry USD or LKR cash.
🌿 Exploring Nature▼
Best time for wildlife safaris at Yala is February–June. Carry insect repellent and binoculars. Early morning gives the best leopard sightings.
🎭 Cultural Etiquette▼
Dress modestly at temples — cover shoulders and knees. Remove shoes before entering. Greet locals with "Ayubowan."
🍛 Food & Dining▼
Try hoppers, kottu roti, and fresh king coconut water. Street food is safe and delicious. Tip 10% at restaurants.
