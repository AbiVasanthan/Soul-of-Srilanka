Complete Travel Guide

Everything you need to know before visiting Sri Lanka

Best Time to Visit

December to March for west coast, April to September for east coast

Budget Guide

$30-50 per day for budget travelers, $100+ for luxury

Visa Requirements

ETA required for most nationalities, apply online before travel

Travel Safety

Generally safe destination, standard precautions advised

Popular Suggested 7-Day Itinerary

Day 1-2: Colombo & Arrival

Colombo

Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport. Explore Colombo's markets, visit Gangaramaya Temple, and enjoy local street food at Pettah Market.

Accommodation: City Hotels Transport: Airport Taxi

Day 3: Sigiriya Rock Fortress

Sigiriya

Travel to Sigiriya (4 hours). Climb the ancient rock fortress, explore frescoes and mirror wall. Visit nearby Dambulla Cave Temple.

Entry: $30 USD Duration: 3-4 hours

Day 4: Kandy - Cultural Capital

Kandy

Visit Temple of the Tooth, explore botanical gardens, watch traditional Kandyan dance performance. Evening walk around Kandy Lake.

Cultural Experience

Day 5: Nine Arches Bridge & Ella

Ella

Scenic train ride to Ella. Visit the iconic Nine Arches Bridge, hike to Little Adam's Peak, enjoy sunset at Ravana Falls.

Train Journey $2-5 USD

Day 6-7: Southern Beaches

Mirissa/Unawatuna

Relax on pristine beaches, whale watching in Mirissa (seasonal), snorkeling, fresh seafood, and sunset cocktails by the ocean.

Beach Activities Departure Day

Essential Travel Tips

Currency: Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). 1 USD ≈ 300 LKR (approximate)

  • ATMs widely available in cities and tourist areas
  • Credit cards accepted at hotels and restaurants
  • Always carry cash for local markets and small shops
  • Tipping: 10% is customary at restaurants

Essentials:
  • Light, breathable clothing
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Sunscreen & hat
  • Insect repellent
  • Universal power adapter (Type D/G)
For Temple Visits:
  • Clothes covering shoulders & knees
  • Scarf or shawl
  • Remove shoes & hats
  • Modest dress required

Recommended Providers: Dialog, Mobitel, Hutch

  • Tourist SIM available at airport: $5-10 for 10-20GB
  • Free WiFi at most hotels and cafes
  • 4G coverage good in cities, limited in rural areas
  • Need passport for SIM registration

  • Drink bottled or filtered water only
  • Vaccinations: Hepatitis A, Typhoid recommended
  • Malaria risk low, but use mosquito repellent
  • Emergency number: 119 (Police), 110 (Ambulance)
  • Travel insurance highly recommended
  • Pharmacies available in all major cities

  • Greet with "Ayubowan" (hands pressed together)
  • Remove shoes before entering temples and homes
  • Don't turn your back to Buddha statues
  • Ask permission before photographing people
  • Dress modestly, especially at religious sites
  • Public displays of affection frowned upon
  • Use right hand for eating and giving/receiving items

Sri Lankan Food Guide
Rice and Curry

The staple dish featuring rice with various curries, sambols, and papadums.

Hoppers (Appa)

Bowl-shaped pancakes made from fermented rice flour, often served with egg.

Kottu Roti

Chopped roti stir-fried with vegetables, eggs, meat, and spices. A street food favorite!

String Hoppers

Steamed rice noodle nests, typically eaten for breakfast with curry.

Dining Tips:
  • Try local restaurants (hotels) for authentic cuisine
  • Spice levels can be adjusted - ask for "mild" if needed
  • Don't miss the fresh tropical fruits: mango, papaya, king coconut
  • Street food is safe if it's freshly cooked and hot

Adventure Activities
Surfing

Best spots: Arugam Bay (East Coast), Hikkaduwa & Weligama (South Coast). Perfect for beginners and pros!

  • Board rental: $5-10/day
  • Lessons: $20-30/hour
  • Best season: April-October (East), November-March (South)
Hiking & Trekking

Popular trails: Adam's Peak, Little Adam's Peak (Ella), Knuckles Mountain Range, Horton Plains.

  • Guide recommended for longer treks
  • Start early morning for best views
  • Bring water and snacks
Wildlife Safari

Top parks: Yala (leopards), Udawalawe (elephants), Minneriya (elephant gathering), Wilpattu.

  • Jeep safari: $40-60
  • Park entry: $15-20
  • Best time: Early morning or late afternoon
Whale Watching

Mirissa is the prime location for spotting blue whales, sperm whales, and dolphins.

  • Tour cost: $30-50
  • Duration: 3-4 hours
  • Season: November-April
  • Success rate: 90%+
Scuba Diving

Best sites: Hikkaduwa, Trincomalee, Nilaveli. See coral reefs, shipwrecks, and marine life.

  • Discover dive: $40-60
  • Certification course: $350-450
  • Fun dive: $30-50
White Water Rafting

Kitulgala is the adventure capital offering grade 2-3 rapids on Kelani River.

  • Half day: $35-50
  • Includes equipment & guide
  • Also offers: Canyoning, cave exploration
Safety Tips:
  • Always use licensed operators and guides
  • Check weather conditions before water activities
  • Travel insurance covering adventure sports recommended
  • Follow all safety instructions
